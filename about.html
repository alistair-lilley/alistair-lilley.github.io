About WDYS? (What Did You See?)

What Did You See? is a horror anthology web novel about a community of ordinary people who face extraordinary supernatural events that leave them traumatized and wanting answers. The story is told as if it were a 1990s/2000s forum, with each forum thread constituting a chapter of the story.
