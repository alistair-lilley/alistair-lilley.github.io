About WDYS? (What Did You See?)

What Did You See? is a horror anthology web novel about a community of ordinary people who face extraordinary supernatural events that leave them traumatized and wanting answers. The story is told as if it were a 199X/200X/201X forum, with each forum thread constituting a chapter of the story.

AI Policy

No amount of the writing in What Did You See? was generated by AI.

A limited amount of AI assistance was used in the implementation of the What Did You See? website, but concept and design were wholly done without the use of AI and only a minute percentage of the HTML and CSS was generated by AI. The usage of AI in this project was limited to assistance with cleaning up parts of the HTML and CSS to make it look more polished and function more cleanly.
