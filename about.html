About WDYS? (What Did You See?)

What Did You See? is a horror anthology web novel written, designed, and implemented by Rhys Raleigh Robbins, about a community of ordinary people who face extraordinary supernatural events that leave them traumatized and wanting answers. The story is told as if it were an early 2000s forum, with each forum thread constituting a chapter of the story.
